Modifications: Macintosh snd resources were decoded to mono WAV files for Godot
playback. Source sample rates below 8000 Hz were deterministically resampled to
8000 Hz because the Godot WAV importer does not accept those original rates.

Per-resource source and output hashes are recorded in
src/presentation/assets/classic-application-media.json.

Classic typography

Black Chancery is the public-domain digitization shipped in the Realmz game
resources. ChicagoFLF is public domain by its designer, Robin Casady. Both are
imported from Realmz Castle at commit
491816ad60037394f92c428e99c004494d3c28b3.

Theldrow-Classic.fnt and Theldrow-Classic.png are deterministic conversions of
the original Realmz FONT 1601 bitmap strike and metrics in the pinned Castle
resource fork. They are used under this project's non-commercial Realmz
artwork license and are not separately licensed for reuse.

Inter is distributed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 and is the open
Geneva substitute selected by Realmz Castle. Its license is included beside
the bundled fonts.

Exact font hashes and accompanying notices are recorded in
src/presentation/assets/fonts/font-assets.json and its licenses directory.
