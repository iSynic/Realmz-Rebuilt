# Presentation workspace controller contract

## Purpose

Own route-local presentation state and control construction behind typed detached views.

## Local Contracts

- Controllers receive an explicit target container and detached values; they never receive `GameSession`, repositories, or the owning router.
- Controllers emit typed intents, host actions, or presentation-setting changes. They never mutate gameplay state.
- `ClassicScreenRouter` alone mounts primary workspaces and restores route focus.
- `ClassicWorkspacePresenter` owns route-local controller composition, detached route state, content rendering, and route-specific audio. The router supplies the mounted route body and navigation-owned back label; it does not render domain content.
- A controller may preserve selection, filtering, sorting, tabs, and draft text for its own route only.
- Application-route controllers own compact task-specific character selection inside their routed workspace. They do not depend on roster-row interaction, and item-local operations such as Trade keep their exact source item and recipient choices together.
- `InventoryWorkspaceController` owns Castle's paper-white, icon-led carried-item ledger, compact selected-character command rail, integrated six-member portrait selector, and full-width selected-item record. Exact CICNs retain an explicit display size; each ledger line may annotate the detached `damage-range` or identified `armor` fact in Castle's red/blue language without recalculating either value. The ordinary ledger uses Black Chancery item names, dark ink, and explicit pale-gold selection. Its Trade state never preselects or presses a row: two independently selected packs flank duplicated six-member portrait columns, Money/Items/Done routes, and a compact item record; only an exact-instance cross-ledger drop submits Trade, and the workspace stays open for subsequent drops. Inventory actions retain their stable command identities but render as uniform Rebuilt raised/inset slate controls instead of importing Castle's inconsistent word-bearing button slabs. The controller may reflow the record vertically in the optional Classic profile, but it keeps exact-instance actions and Trade staging together and never calculates character or item facts.
- `ServicesWorkspaceController` owns the ordinary Party Wealth route, not a catalog of hypothetical services. Wide composition separates the detached party pool, six adventurer wealth records, and selected-character Swap controls; Classic composition condenses character selection into the Swap pane. Pool, Share, denomination increments, load facts, and disabled reasons remain core-owned, while Done and character selection are presentation-owned.
- `SpellsWorkspaceController` uses a persistent right sidebar wider than the ordinary roster—420 pixels canonically and 288 pixels in the optional Classic profile—from the menu to the window bottom while preserving the map and narrative footer to its left. Both profiles keep the exact gradient `Spell Level` vertical rail and seven compact power choices beside 21-pixel same-level rows, a bounded one-line selected description, and four-column facts. Cast and Make Scroll mount in the route's fixed context-action strip beside Back rather than inside either the list or the route scroll. `Fast Spells (1–0)` names Castle's ten character-owned number-key bindings rather than implying a spell-speed class; each bounded row assigns immediately when its picker changes, exposes only a separate Clear action, and survives Back through the resulting typed session rerender. Scroll Case remains separate and renders detached Use and Discard actions for each occupied slot; both emit the same typed scroll intent because the session owns the exact cast-versus-discard interaction. The ordinary record uses the shared native-slate target badge only for catalog-proven gradient donor lettering; unproven target types remain ordinary semantic facts, and a combat-queue CICN is never treated as the spell's menu identity. The controller may format supplied scaling, target, resistance, save, cost, and availability facts but cannot infer legal powers, targets, effects, or resource identities.
- `CreatureLibraryWorkspaceController` owns read-only selection and rendering for current held-over allies. It consumes detached `MonsterView` records and exact media keys; it never constructs combat state, changes ally membership, or treats current allies as the Bestiary denominator. Wide composition uses one backed current-allies list beside a dominant record, while the optional Classic profile stacks those same panes so facts remain legible. List and detail art must resolve the ally's exact CICN resource key with nearest-neighbor sampling; unavailable media stays visibly absent rather than substituting a generic creature.
- `MapsJournalWorkspaceController` owns three route-local backed workspaces: selectable saved/current location notes with a restrained parchment editor, acquired player maps on a low-luminance map table, and scrollable two-page parchment journal records with a leather spine and dark ink. Saved-note selection renders only the supplied detached historical `MapView`, including its saved darkness intensity, while the editor remains bound to the current location. The route screen is the sole heading owner; the controller adds only counts, selectors, bounded record previews, presentation-only authored-journal search, and presentation-only player-map zoom. It preserves tab, selected-record, filter, and zoom state across selection rerenders, emits only the typed current-location note intent, and centers player maps through `PlayerMapPresenter` on `PlayerMapCartographicStage`; it never acquires a map, edits authored journal text, changes map truth, or invents thumbnails for missing media.
- `SystemWorkspaceController` exposes the host-owned traveled-area preview and Auto Note toggles in Controls plus the default-on Classic exploration-distance policy in Display. All persist only through `PresentationSettings`; map preferences change rendering from detached visited facts and Auto Note selects the acknowledgement payload, without mutating simulation directly. Its Audio tab keeps master, effects, and music volumes separate, persists global music enablement, and opens the shell-owned playlist modal without owning playback. Controls also owns the fixed keyboard/mouse reference derived from the public input contract; it does not imply unsupported remapping.
- `SystemWorkspaceController` keeps Save & Load separate from Display, Audio, Accessibility, Controls, and Diagnostics. Every preference tab uses a backed, vertically scrollable slate card so 800×600 at maximum text scale remains reachable; rows align wide and stack in the compact `UiLayoutProfile`. The route screen is the sole title owner, and the controller adds only a bounded campaign/rules context line. Display pick lists use the shared Rebuilt-font option role in either typography mode. The save workspace owns slot/source selection, one detached preview, and fixed host actions: Quick Save retains the stable `quick` slot, Save Selected overwrites the selected slot through the existing host boundary, and Save New Slot accepts only the repository's portable slot alphabet. A host-requested Save and Quit mode replaces ordinary save controls with one selected-slot Save and Quit action, while Back cancels that host mode. It distinguishes primary and backup loads and leaves corrupt/incompatible records visible with their exact reason. Preference tabs emit only existing typed presentation-setting changes and apply immediately; they never alter Classic rules or invent Apply/Reset, deletion, screenshots, schema mutation, or migration controls absent from the host contracts.
- `CampaignLibraryController` owns the Black Chancery front-door composition, licensed looping application animation and its separately looping click-enabled soundtrack inside the scalable app-owned ornamental frame, new/saved-adventure entry actions, fast manifest/readiness rows, selected `CampaignSummaryView` record, and one fixed package-operation host. It activates the uncached intro streams only while the splash is visible and releases both on every exit; the embedded video audio is never enabled. It may resolve only the selected summary's exact `splashAssetId`; it cannot decode package content during discovery, classify free-form readiness errors, or retain terminal host failures absent from its detached operation view. External installation starts from one host file picker filtered to `.realmz2`; no developer path field appears in the ordinary library. Package progress disables competing install/refresh/scenario actions without displacing party controls. `CampaignPartySetupController` composes explicit shared setup state with independent inspection, party-assembly, and five-step creation controllers; its Load Saved Adventure action emits only a presentation route request to the package-bound System workspace. Those collaborators never inherit behavior from one another or receive the router. The facade preserves construction, visibility, focus, and its public signal/method boundary. Both public controllers mount under an explicit overlay host supplied by the shell scene.
- `PartySetupCharacterCreationController` owns the full-stage five-step local draft surface. Identity, Race/Caste, Appearance, Review, and Spells may retain only presentation-local selections before emitting typed intents. Race drives the left/right selector relationship; each Race and Caste record pairs a narrow pressed selector with a wider detached detail, named-compatibility pane, and source-backed rule facts. Empty source descriptions occupy no UI and never become invented fallback prose. The controller may suppress only literal numbered fallback labels and may sort compatible Castes before incompatible records, but it cannot infer compiler provenance or calculate modifiers. Appearance exposes exact package portrait/tactical catalogs as paged two-row, six-column grids labelled from explicit `recommended_race_ids`; it never infers grouping from labels or numeric ranges. Portrait selection may follow the proven Classic resource pairing until the player independently overrides the tactical icon. Review consumes the detached provisional `CharacterView` through the shared complete character sheet; it may resolve that view's exact appearance and item media, but cannot calculate rules, invent restrictions, or substitute resource identities. Starting Spells shares `ClassicSpellSelectionChrome` with the ordinary spellbook, keeps only presentation-local level/focus state, and emits the complete typed selected-ID set so choices on hidden levels survive filtering. Pressed state communicates selection without modifier-key helper copy. `CharacterSpellOptionView` supplies the exact Classic resolution-effect resource sequence; the creator may animate only those IDs through scenario-first application-media resolution and must not derive art from a numeric spell ID. Actions remain outside the scrolling record body.

## Verification

- Route lifecycle tests own primary-workspace exclusivity and modal input ownership; do not add one test per overlap symptom.
