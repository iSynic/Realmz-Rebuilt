```powershell
./tools/audit_macro_spell_reachability.ps1 src/storage/packages/bundled_campaigns -OutputPath "$env:TEMP/realmz-macro-audit.json"
```

## Execution and source limits

- Immediate areas, persistent fields and repeated-target rows share the deterministic spell resolver. Repeated-target selection retains FD-SCENARIO-009.
- Target types 1 and 6 use the bounded source-cell handoff in FD-SCENARIO-016. Type 1 avoids Castle's unsafe coordinate-to-actor alias; type 6 preserves its point footprint without inventing a ray endpoint.
- Other recognized but unimplemented target families remain explicit implementation failures when selected. Static storage alone does not prove they execute from a valid gameplay caller.
- AOGM XAPs 165 and 169 are now present. Their previous absence was a compiler/package preservation defect, not missing authored content.
- Absent targets exposed by formerly excluded Battle/Monster ranges remain authored operands, including White Dragon XAP 9363. Their source bytes and rows are retained; no target is invented. Independent native accounting distinguishes source absence from a compilation omission.
- Controlled Rebuilt fixtures and public VM tests exercise representative area, queued, repeated and source-cell paths. They do not establish full route reachability or controlled Castle runtime parity.
- The complete forty-folder D:/Scenarios corpus and the separate twenty-five pinned source revisions compile and pass independent native accounting and Rebuilt admission in isolated outputs. This includes unfinished and previously unimported campaigns; the twelve additional differing private source sets retain separate identities. Those facts do not certify macro execution or campaign completion, and private payloads are not shipped.
