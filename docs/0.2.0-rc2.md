# Realmz Rebuilt 0.2 Release Candidate 2

Release Candidate 2 fixes numpad attacks after you fire a missile or switch to your missile weapon: stepping into an adjacent enemy attacks again. Everything else is the same as Release Candidate 1, including the new scenario runner and save v9.

[Downloads for Windows, Linux, and macOS](https://github.com/iSynic/Realmz-Rebuilt/releases). Choose Release Candidate 2 explicitly, extract the complete archive, and keep its importer and music-importer folders with the game. This is an opt-in prerelease with desktop builds only; no Android APK is included. **Beta 27 remains the Latest release and the fallback.**

## Combat fix

- **Moving into an enemy attacks in missile mode** ([#27](https://github.com/iSynic/Realmz-Rebuilt/issues/27)). As in the Realmz Castle codebase, a numpad or click move into an adjacent enemy now attacks with your melee weapon, or unarmed, even while you are set to missile. Your weapon mode and ammunition stay as they were, so your next Fire still uses the missile.
- **Point-blank thrown weapons keep their rule.** Thrown weapons whose effect is class 9 with damage type 9 still need Auto Weapon Switch to step into melee. With it off, you get the switch-weapon warning, as in Castle.
- **Attack Friend follows the same rule.** Moving into an ally still offers Swap Positions or Attack Friend while you have more than 4 movement left.

## Saves and returning to Beta 27

Nothing changed here since Release Candidate 1. **Release Candidate 2 writes save v9 and reads v5–v9. Beta 27 cannot read v9.** Keep an untouched v8-or-earlier save if you want to return to Beta 27. Both versions use the same local data folder, so back up saves and characters you care about before testing.

Character Files, the application library, the bundled scenario archives and the package formats are unchanged. No reimport or Update Save step is needed.

## What to test and report

Please keep testing the new scenario runner as described in the [Release Candidate 1 notes](beta-28.md): encounters, scripted battles, death and revival, timed events, and saving and restoring while a prompt is open. For combat, try firing a missile and then stepping into an enemy, both with and without Auto Weapon Switch.

Use the [beta bug report form](https://github.com/iSynic/Realmz-Rebuilt/issues/new?template=beta_bug.yml). Include the version, operating system, scenario, exact steps, and an original save from before the problem when possible. Check attachments for private information before sharing them.

## Known limitations

The intermittent combat spellbook-input issue remains unresolved: Power Level, Choose Targets, and Abort can sometimes stop responding while Back to battle still works. Returning to battle and reopening Spells may help.

The optional modular HUD remains a work in progress; the fixed HUD is still the default. Verification covers the documented execution, restoration, legacy-save, campaign-caller and combat-collision cases. It does not certify every route through every campaign.
