- `src/realmz_orig/attack.c:695-697,785-793,976-998,1201-1222` resolves ordinary monster damage before special processing, consumes a generic special-potency draw for every nonzero special, and routes party-target special 17 through save slot seven. - `src/realmz_orig/attack.c:785-889,1011-1084,1224-1229` and `misc.c:1188-1229` establish monster status specials 1–7 and 16. The shared potency draw precedes every save. Party conditions use the pinned Castle correction: a failed save adds potency once when the starting duration is below 30, may exceed 30, still reports a result from 30 or above, and cannot replace a negative permanent sentinel. Monster conditions have no duration gate; their save-seven chance is the integer average of six saves, while other save families retain the shifted save byte, spell immunity, and undead auto-save after consuming the roll. Runtime monster magic resistance above 100 jumps to the attack's whiff path after potency, removing ordinary damage. Party status feedback uses sound 630; monster disease uses 684. Differential case `combat.monster-status-specials` covers the exact table, draw order, state branches, sound/event order, and persistence.
- `src/realmz_orig/attack.c:785-917,1086-1097,1224-1229`, `misc.c:1188-1229`, and `structs.h:150-171,300-326` establish resource specials 8 and 9. Special 8 then tests save six before checking spell points, transfers the smaller of the target balance or hit dice times three, and does not clamp the attacker's signed-short balance to `maxspellpoints`; it applies to party and monster targets without a sound. There is no monster-target case 9 and therefore no save draw there. - `src/realmz_orig/attack.c:918-1179`, `misc.c:1188-1229`, `checkforenemy.c:5-75`, `getup.c:205-214`, and `booty.c:94-111` establish the remaining monster specials.
