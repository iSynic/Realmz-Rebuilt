# Realmz Rebuilt 0.2.3

Realmz Rebuilt 0.2.3 closes the mountain shortcuts in Trouble in the Sword Lands that let you walk around the story, lets charmed and confused characters use their missile weapons, keeps the Encounter Skills and Items panels from changing size, and hides the HUD button titles by default.

[Downloads for Windows, Linux, and macOS](https://github.com/iSynic/Realmz-Rebuilt/releases). Extract the complete archive and keep its importer and music-importer folders with the game. This release contains desktop builds only; no Android APK is included.

## Fixes

- **Trouble in the Sword Lands no longer has mountain shortcuts** ([#38](https://github.com/iSynic/Realmz-Rebuilt/issues/38)). Some mountain tiles along the edges of three maps could be walked over, so a party could cross between regions and skip parts of the story. I've corrected 75 tiles: those mountains now block movement, and three paths move one tile inward so they stay open. This is my own correction to the scenario, not a change to how the Realmz Castle codebase plays it.
- **Charmed and confused characters use their missile weapons.** A character who fights on their own now throws or fires a readied missile weapon at an enemy who isn't next to them, including ammunition that carries a spell, before walking up to melee. Each shot uses up ammunition and costs no movement, as in the Realmz Castle codebase. They still don't cast spells from their spellbook, which Castle doesn't do either.
- **Encounter Skills and Items keep their size.** After you used a skill or an item in an encounter, the panel could resize itself and the Thief portrait could be drawn at the wrong scale. Both now stay at the size your interface settings call for.

## Changes

- **HUD button titles are off by default.** The setting is now called Show HUD button titles, on the Interface page of Settings. New and reset settings hide the captions under the exploration buttons, and the tooltips still name every button. If you already chose to show or hide them, your choice stays.

## Saves

**0.2.3 writes the same save v9 as 0.2.2 and reads v5–v9.** Beta 27 can't read v9, so keep an untouched v8-or-earlier save if you want to be able to go back to it.

The Trouble in the Sword Lands package changed. To keep playing a Trouble save from 0.2.2 or earlier, select it in your saved adventures and choose **Update Save**. It writes an updated copy next to your original and leaves the original untouched. Your party, progress and position stay exactly as they were. If your party was saved standing on one of the newly blocked mountain tiles, it stays there; you can usually step off onto a neighboring tile, but in a few spots along one map's east edge you may be boxed in, so load an earlier save from before you crossed if that happens.

Character Files v2, the application library and every other bundled scenario haven't changed, so you don't need to reimport anything or update saves from other scenarios.

All versions use the same local data folder, so extracting another copy of the game doesn't give it separate saves or Character Files. Back up saves and characters you care about before upgrading.

## Reporting problems

Use the [bug report form](https://github.com/iSynic/Realmz-Rebuilt/issues/new?template=beta_bug.yml). Include the version, operating system, scenario, exact steps, and an original save from before the problem when possible. Check attachments for private information before sharing them.

## Known limitations

Charmed and confused characters don't fire ammunition whose spell hits an area or a line; they walk up and use melee instead. They also aim at the target they already had, or the first enemy in range they can see, rather than picking a random enemy in range the way Castle does.

The intermittent combat spellbook-input issue remains unresolved. Power Level, Choose Targets and Abort can sometimes stop responding while Back to battle still works. Returning to battle and reopening Spells may help.

The optional modular HUD is still a work in progress, and the fixed HUD remains the default.
