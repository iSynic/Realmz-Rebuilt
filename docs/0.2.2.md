# Realmz Rebuilt 0.2.2

Realmz Rebuilt 0.2.2 fixes two combat problems you reported in 0.2.1. Characters who are helpless, charmed, frightened or confused now act the way they do in the Realmz Castle codebase, and creatures no longer hide the area you're targeting.

[Downloads for Windows, Linux, and macOS](https://github.com/iSynic/Realmz-Rebuilt/releases). Extract the complete archive and keep its importer and music-importer folders with the game. This release contains desktop builds only; no Android APK is included.

## Fixes

- **Mental effects work in combat** ([#36](https://github.com/iSynic/Realmz-Rebuilt/issues/36)). These now follow the Realmz Castle codebase:
  - A helpless character loses their turn.
  - A charmed character keeps attacking until they run out of attacks, instead of stopping after one.
  - A frightened party member runs from the enemy on their own. When they reach the edge of the battlefield, you get control back and can choose whether to flee or stay and fight.
  - Confusion works on both sides again. Each turn, a confused party member or monster may switch sides, come back to their own side, become frightened or become helpless.
- **You can see spell areas and targets over creatures** ([#37](https://github.com/iSynic/Realmz-Rebuilt/issues/37)). Area outlines, summon footprints and target outlines now draw on top of creatures and their backgrounds, so a large monster no longer hides the area you're aiming at.

## Saves

**0.2.2 writes the same save v9 as 0.2.1 and reads v5–v9.** Beta 27 can't read v9, so keep an untouched v8-or-earlier save if you want to be able to go back to it.

Character Files v2, the application library, the bundled scenario archives and the package formats haven't changed, so you don't need to reimport anything or use Update Save.

All versions use the same local data folder, so extracting another copy of the game doesn't give it separate saves or Character Files. Back up saves and characters you care about before upgrading.

## Reporting problems

Use the [bug report form](https://github.com/iSynic/Realmz-Rebuilt/issues/new?template=beta_bug.yml). Include the version, operating system, scenario, exact steps, and an original save from before the problem when possible. Check attachments for private information before sharing them.

## Known limitations

Charmed and confused characters who attack on their own still only use melee. In the Realmz Castle codebase they can also cast spells or use missile weapons.

The intermittent combat spellbook-input issue remains unresolved. Power Level, Choose Targets and Abort can sometimes stop responding while Back to battle still works. Returning to battle and reopening Spells may help.

The optional modular HUD is still a work in progress, and the fixed HUD remains the default.
