# Realmz Rebuilt 0.2.4

Realmz Rebuilt 0.2.4 stops the Volcanic Rockies rumble in Trouble in the Sword Lands from repeating, makes frightened characters run as they should, brings more of combat in line with the Realmz Castle codebase, and updates your older saves by itself, so there's no Update Save step anymore.

[Downloads for Windows, Linux, and macOS](https://github.com/iSynic/Realmz-Rebuilt/releases). Extract the complete archive and keep its importer and music-importer folders with the game. This release contains desktop builds only; no Android APK is included.

## Fixes

- **The Volcanic Rockies rumble plays once** ([#39](https://github.com/iSynic/Realmz-Rebuilt/issues/39)). In Trouble in the Sword Lands, the volcano's rumble kept going off every few dozen steps for as long as you stayed on that map. Now you hear it once, and it stays quiet after that, across saves and loads. If your party is already there in an older save, you'll hear it one last time. This is my own correction to the scenario, not a change to how the Realmz Castle codebase plays it.
- **Frightened characters run the way they do in the Realmz Castle codebase, and the battlefield edge asks you first.** A frightened party member steps away from the enemy, takes the parting blows as it goes, and still shoots when it can. Characters acting on their own also bandage a bleeding friend and turn undead as Castle does. When a fleeing character reaches the edge of the battlefield, you choose Embrace Cowardice or Stay and Fight.
- **Battles nobody fights end on their own.** When ten rounds in a row end without anyone on either side attacking, shooting or casting, the battle ends and the party leaves it without treasure, as it does in the Realmz Castle codebase.
- **Missiles work like they do in the Realmz Castle codebase.** You can aim a missile weapon at anyone in range and sight, your own party included. Charmed and confused characters can now fire every kind of magical ammunition except summoning, and its area and ray effects land where Castle puts them.
- **Charmed characters pick and lose targets the same way.** A character charmed to the other side forgets its old target, and a charmed character interrupted by a guard's blow mid-step finishes its move the way Castle does. A character who flees the field alive no longer counts as a kill.

## Changes

- **Saves update by themselves.** When a scenario is corrected, a save from its earlier version updates the first time it shows up in your saved adventures, or when you load it. The updated save keeps its slot and Quick Save still finds it. Your original becomes that slot's backup, and an older backup is kept beside it. Your party, progress and position stay exactly as they were, and a short notice tells you when it happens.

## Saves

**0.2.4 writes the same save v9 as 0.2.3 and reads v5–v9.** Beta 27 can't read v9, so keep an untouched v8-or-earlier save if you want to be able to go back to it.

Only the Trouble in the Sword Lands package changed. Trouble saves from 0.2.3 and earlier update automatically; the [0.2.3 notes](0.2.3.md) cover the mountain tiles for saves from 0.2.2 and earlier. Character Files v2, the application library and every other bundled scenario haven't changed, so you don't need to reimport anything.

All versions use the same local data folder, so extracting another copy of the game doesn't give it separate saves or Character Files. Back up saves and characters you care about before upgrading.

## Reporting problems

Use the [bug report form](https://github.com/iSynic/Realmz-Rebuilt/issues/new?template=beta_bug.yml). Include the version, operating system, scenario, exact steps, and an original save from before the problem when possible. Check attachments for private information before sharing them.

## Known limitations

The intermittent combat spellbook-input issue remains unresolved. Power Level, Choose Targets and Abort can sometimes stop responding while Back to battle still works. Returning to battle and reopening Spells may help.

The optional modular HUD is still a work in progress, and the fixed HUD remains the default.
