## Current character-melee boundary

The implemented melee subset builds a validated `CharacterCombatEquipment` snapshot from immutable item definitions. An equipped Classic type-2 item is the weapon even when its magic plus is zero; damage bonuses on rings or armor continue to contribute without changing an unarmed character into an armed one. The resolver covers equipped damage/luck/armor, weapon condition prechecks, hit chance, type hatred, reflection, weapon physical and elemental ranges, target-tag bonuses, helpless damage, and the two post-damage critical RNG draws. `FD-COMBAT-002` uses the defending monster's save for weapon elemental mitigation.

This is not complete tactical combat. The package now exposes `requiredWeapon` and `magicToHit` independently from battle placement `distance`; combat enforces magical-plus, blunt, sharp, and corrected specific Item Number requirements before auto-hit. The later active-turn cadence case owns attack half-unit preparation. The separate character `spec[15]` ability table, fumble preference/recovery, missile toggling, and full wear/remove side effects are not inferred or claimed by the melee-resolution case.

### Weapon mode and tactical boundary

Castle `combatsetup`, `combatchoice`, `toweap`, `tomissle`, and `wear` establish one battle-local character toggle over distinct type-2 melee, type-10 ammunition, and type-15 missile slots. Melee is the setup default when present; missile is selected only when melee is absent. The active character can toggle without spending an attack, movement, or the turn, and melee mode remains valid as hand-to-hand without a type-2 item. Realmz 2.0 now owns that state in `CombatState`, restores older save-v3 battle data to the safe melee default, rejects duplicate Classic slots, and carries the exact legal actions through `CombatView` and the typed battle interaction.

This does not implement projectile combat. Battle setup now constructs and saves Castle-shaped terrain, positions, and footprints, but adjacency, Euclidean range, line of sight, movement, item/spell projectile identity, projectile-specific saves, and exact charge and action costs remain unimplemented. Schema v2 preserves Data BD distance, authored monster coordinates, each active landlook's exact 0-through-200 mapstats, and the global 200-through-400 combat table; the runtime independently rejects incomplete maps, malformed terrain builds, wrong map references, and malformed IDs. Character Fire and monster missile resolution still report an explicit unavailable state, and a monster missile choice cannot impersonate physical attack row zero. Current 2.0 melee targets remain unfiltered by proximity and are not Classic tactical proof.

Castle's positive `behind` flag belongs to withdrawal attacks from `checkforenemy(2)`: every hostile left adjacent by the move receives a temporary +20. Character `face` and monster `lr` only select icon orientation. Remake's adjacency comparison is a useful lead, but its consumed once-per-round `reaction_ready` is not Castle behavior. No facing, flanking, or backstab mechanic is inferred from those rendering fields.
