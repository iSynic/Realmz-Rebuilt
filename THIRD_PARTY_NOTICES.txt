src/presentation/assets/classic-application-media.json.

Classic typography

Black Chancery is the public-domain digitization shipped in the Realmz game
resources. ChicagoFLF is public domain by its designer, Robin Casady. Both are
imported from Realmz Castle at commit
491816ad60037394f92c428e99c004494d3c28b3.

Theldrow-Classic.fnt and Theldrow-Classic.png are deterministic conversions of
the original Realmz FONT 1601 bitmap strike and metrics in the pinned Castle
resource fork. They remain native-size reference assets under this project's
non-commercial Realmz artwork license and are not separately licensed for reuse.

Theldrow-Classic-Vector.ttf is derived from Samuel/iSynic's scalable
theldrowremake.ttf glyph outlines at Realmz Remake commit
86cf2bf391ef0c43ba31c1633ddd63b7e67e3d61. Those outlines are a CC0
FontStruction by wasikinghit. Realmz Rebuilt deterministically replaces their
horizontal advances with the original Realmz FONT 1601 advances so scalable
text retains Classic spacing. The CC0 license and readme are included beside
the bundled fonts.

Inter is distributed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 and is the open
Geneva substitute selected by Realmz Castle. Its license is included beside
the bundled fonts.

Exact font hashes and accompanying notices are recorded in
src/presentation/assets/fonts/font-assets.json and its licenses directory.

Realmz intro animation

The looping front-door animation is a deterministic half-size, temporally
sampled conversion of the Realmz intro GIF supplied by the project owner.
The source GIF SHA-256 is
43dd46139afab9f63f08b782781d476c91cf3a7bc21b7b8ae69b7ea47ac7f3fb.
It is used under this project's non-commercial Realmz artwork license.
Per-frame hashes and preserved timing are recorded in
src/presentation/assets/ui/intro/intro-animation.json.
