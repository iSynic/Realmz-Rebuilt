# Economy and services

Start in `src/game/economy`. `RealmzContent.economy` exposes the immutable `EconomyContentCatalog` for Shop and Treasure definitions. `EconomyRules` owns denominations, carrying weight, valuation, Pool, Share, and Swap. `TempleRules` owns the nine temple services. `LocationServiceState` owns the current shop, temple, and bank plus mutable shop stock; `LocationServiceStateCodec` keeps that state in the existing flat `GameState` save dictionary. Shops, temples, and banks may arrive either as scenario interactions or contextual services, but their mutations converge through these owners and the public session boundary.

`EconomyIntents.money` and `EconomyIntents.service` are the player-command entry points. Their typed payloads carry only the selected operation, character, denomination, service, and amount; affordability and mutation remain in the owning workflow and rules. Session-level money and service resumption live together under `src/playthrough/economy`.

`ServiceContinuations` owns the resumable service and pooled-wealth departure handoffs beside `SessionMoneyWorkflow`. `ServiceContinuationBody` carries only the active service continuation or the exact departure stage and direction; the shared saved envelope does not implement economy behavior. `SessionResponsesCoordinator` dispatches departure through named Warning and Distribution phases, then delegates Pool, Share, Swap, or leave-behind mutation to one readable operation before resuming movement.

Presentation lives together under `src/ui/services`; its local README is the visual-maintainer entry point for Party Wealth, Shop, Temple, Bank, and Treasure. It renders the detached records in `src/game/economy`—prices, balances, load, and availability—and never recalculates affordability. The public regression owner is `tests/integration/test_money_workflow.gd`; persistence and scenario restoration are covered by `test_session_persistence.gd` and `test_scenario_vm.gd`. Stable service layout belongs in the owning scenes, with scripts limited to binding supplied records and typed responses.

Scenario rewards enter through `ClassicRewardOperations`. Follow terminal combat settlement and recovered fumbles into `ClassicBattleRewardBuilder`: it validates one complete reward plan before ownership or RNG advances, then rolls money, monster loot, and incidental items in source order. `ClassicRewardWorkflow` opens that plan through named scaling, item-admission, item-instance, and experience-award phases; resumed Treasure actions retain separate completion, custody, wealth, lore, level, and spell-selection operations. Their shared rollback and battle-message policy lives in `ClassicRewardOperationsSupport`. Reward behavior is characterized by `tests/scenario/test_reward_workflow.gd` and persistence coverage in `tests/integration/test_session_persistence.gd`.

The Shop begins at `src/ui/services/shop_interaction.tscn`. Both its wide two-ledger arrangement and compact tabbed arrangement are visible there, along with the complete transaction footer and detail wells. `shop_interaction.gd` selects one authored profile, binds request-owned values, and instantiates only `shop_item_row.tscn`, `shop_character_button.tscn`, and the explicit empty-state row for variable collections. `ClassicServiceOperations.shop_request` composes separately named stock and character-inventory projections; its resume path dispatches directly to Buy, Sell, Identify, or Leave while preserving one regenerated typed request after each successful transaction.
